## Leadership

An individual is an active organism. His operating system is his character.  His thoughts, words and deeds are products of his character. Therefore, the basis of leadership is character.

> The supreme quality of leadership is integrity.  
> **Dwight Eisenhower**

> Character protects the leaders life, his leadership and his legacy.  
> It manifests who he is and shapes who he will become.  
> Without character, every other aspect of leadership is at risk.  
> **Myles Munroe**

> The greatest among you must be a servant.  
> **Matthew 23:11 NLT**

Successful living is called leading a good life. You need to be able to lead yourself well. You also need to work in collaboration with others and be able to tap into their unique strengths. If each person is unique there should not be competition, only cooperation.

People are born with different gifts and they get nurtured differently. Competition for the same thing — resources, power and the spotlight — often gets in the way. Good character development with the proper nurturing of God given gifts results in discipline and prosperity.

In the middle ages there was a system of master and apprentice for nurturing talent. In Christianity this is termed shepherds and disciples. You must remember that the shepherds and disciples lead normal lives among the people. They get married and have families.  They are not monks in a monastery. They look to Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior and are guided by the Word of God in the Bible. Jesus Christ is the King of Kings.

> For there is one God and one mediator between God and mankind, the man Christ Jesus.  
> **1 Timothy 2:5 NIV**

While a wise and capable individual must rule a country, he or she can’t be the only wise and capable one. We all die eventually and different circumstances require different capabilities in the leadership. For this reason, we must continually groom leaders at all levels. From the heads of households to any other organization and collective and collaborative effort all the way up to the Commander in Chief.

> A culture evolves by figuring out better ways of life — to include more and more people into the better life.  
> The purpose is to lift each individual up to his or her calling.  
> **Ruchira Bomiriya**
