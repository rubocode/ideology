## Policy

We have been fighting for peace for decades.  It will never come through conquest and victory.  It will come through wisdom and compassion.  What we must remember is that the goal is peace, not victory.  Victory does not guarantee peace, but peace itself is victory.

> සබ්බපාපස්ස අකරණං, කුසලස්ස උපසම්පදා  
> සචිත්ත පරියෝදපනං, ඒතං බුද්ධානුසාසනං.  
> Refraining from all evil; attainment of what is good;  
> cleansing of one’s thoughts;  This is the advice of the Awakened Ones.  
> **The Dhammapada (Verse 183)**

> Depart from evil and do good;  
> Seek peace and pursue it.  
> **Psalm 34:14 NKJV**

We need to remain a peaceful country internationally and our foreign policy needs to reflect that.  We need to stand up for what is right, at least on a case by case basis.  Nonalignment is a timid cop out.  Sovereignty is the courageous path. If we are to deal peacefully and intelligently in international affairs, we need to understand the rest of the world.  This means creating global citizens who have depth and breadth. _**Our education must be globalist**_.

We need the world to value a Sri Lankan in any given situation.  We should have that unique brand that distinguishes us internationally.  And what better brand to have than _**talented**_, _**intelligent**_ and _**peaceful**_.  That’s the way to arm ourselves for peace.  The right education is of paramount importance.

People must be educated in what is valuable in the here and now as well as in what is timeless and enduring.  Psychology, Philosophy, History and Economics are important in the future where simple survival skills and knowledge will be replaced by machines, robots and artificial intelligence.

> You can arrive at what you think is the truth, but you need confirmation.  
> You need to be able to judge whether you have arrived.  
> You’re going to need an internal compass.  
> **Ruchira Bomiriya**

We must maintain our unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity and take advantage of our geopolitical legacy and destiny as an independent maritime hub with a unique enduring gene pool coupled with a rich history and culture.
