## Strategy

In 1993, I was in my third year at Stevens Institute of Technology and had been working as a co-op student at Maidenform, Inc. for more than two years. I was a programmer figuring out how to recognize different patterns of lace and cutting them.  The challenging part was the pattern recognition.

I had recognized that I was trying to get a computer — a complete moron — to learn how to learn.  One of my friends had handed me a copy of the book The Society of Mind written by Marvin Minsky who happened to be a co-founder of the Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence at Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT).

Minsky was trying to describe how the neurons in the brain which are almost essentially the same demonstrate quite miraculous capabilities by the way they are specialized and organized.  This ignited a spark in me.  This is the same situation as humans who are quite similar and are organized on Earth.  It was the governance that enabled stability and progress.

The answer I came across was alignment from grassroots to the ivory towers.  If there was an absolute truth, this becomes possible.  An operating system available to each human that is able to tap into to his or her full potential providing peace, freedom and liberation.  The strategy was to empower people with the Truth — both the rulers and the citizens.

Here is an extract from the notes I had made on September 12, 1993 at 4:30am.  It was a three page document that I still possess.  I had added the “and vice-versa” addition on December 12, 1998.

![photo](/ideology/photos/strategy.png)

No good enduring thing is produced as a result of hatred an urgency.  Individuals learn, grow and mature over time. So do cultures. It is the duty and obligation of governance to guide progress under peace and stability and appropriate correction.

The top has to be clean for the bottom to follow.  But it is from the bottom that we promote people to the top.  Therefore, the bottom has to be clean to produce clean governance.  This can be achieved only under alignment with the absolute truth.
