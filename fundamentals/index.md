## Fundamentals

There are five fundamental roots humans have used as the basis of their cultures and belief systems. These are closely knit to a core question. Is there an absolute truth? and what is that truth?

The issue is complicated by the allowance for the supernatural. Is there more to life beyond what we experience through sensory perception? Is this life on Earth all that there is to it or is it merely a temporary dwelling that is part of a longer journey?

The most crucial question is how we should live here. What is our purpose here? Even the people who deny the supernatural have to have a good answer to the question of purpose. Do you make up a purpose casually out of thin air?
The five fundamental roots result in five types of people:

* There are many gods (polytheist)
* There is one God (monotheist)
* There are no gods (atheist)
* It is not possible to find out (agnostic)
* Let’s see what I will find (seeker)

The last one is a little different from the other four. The other four hold rigidly to their belief and journey. The seeker is willing to change his mind and may possibly end up in one of the four categories or even simply continue to stay a seeker.

I was born into a Buddhist family in Colombo, Sri Lanka and I was a practicing Buddhist.  In my late forties, I went through a combination of hardships and challenges and an accidental meeting with a Christian pastor helped me overcome the spiritual challenges and helped me put my life back on track.

> Ask, and it shall be given you;  
> seek, and ye shall find;  
> knock, and it shall be opened unto you:  
> **Matthew 7:7 KJV**

I am now 56 years old and my journey has led me to the point where I can assuredly state that there is purpose in life and that it is not a crap shoot. I have faith and confidence in the one God of Christianity. The reason I disclosed my own personal belief here is so that the Christian vocabulary enables me to explain what I am trying to get across in straightforward fashion with clarity and precision instead of leisurely philosophizing to make everyone happy.
