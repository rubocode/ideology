## Idolatry

In the Bible, Egypt is special. God’s children were working as slaves in Egypt — essentially working for money and not for God.

> And God spoke all these words:  
> I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery.  
> You shall have no other gods before me.  
> You shall not make for yourself an image in the form of anything in heaven above or on the earth beneath or in the waters below.  
> You shall not bow down to them or worship them; for I, the Lord your God, am a jealous God, punishing the children for the sin of the parents to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me, but showing love to a thousand generations of those who love me and keep my commandments.  
> **Exodus 20:1-6 NIV**

In this sense, we say that you’re in Egypt if you’re essentially spending your life away working for and chasing money and not living by the Truth in freedom.

> No one can serve two masters.  
> For you will hate one and love the other; you will be devoted to one and despise the other.  
> You cannot serve God and be enslaved to money.  
> **Matthew 6:24 NLT**

God forbids the worship of idols. There is one true God and anyone else claiming to be a god is an idol and does not represent the Truth. This is analogous to the Right View (සම්‍යක් දෘෂ්ටිය) against any Incorrect View (මිථ්‍යා දෘෂ්ටිය) or delusion (මායාවට හසුවීම) in Buddhism. View here stands for vision, belief and perspective.

In my personal situation, it was not that I was convinced that Buddhism was wrong, it was just that practically and in real life I was saved by faith in Christ Jesus. Buddhism sometimes is claimed not to be a religion but a philosophy. It is not my intention to set out to find what if anything is wrong or missing in Buddhism. It is a path and I also took it for 49 years.  There are truths in it that I still value and apply in practice. I have journeyed in life in search of the Truth as a student and not as a master.  Now, I am a disciple of Jesus Christ.

> These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace.  
> In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer;  
> I have overcome the world.  
> **John 16:33 KJV**

Sometimes I think of the situation as the difference between being a civilian and being in the special forces.  Focus, accuracy and strict discipline are essential in the latter.
