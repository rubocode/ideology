## Vision

Right from the beginning to the end of my school career, the education and the zeitgeist of the country as a whole was focused on _development_. The vision was to create a Righteous Society (ධර්මිෂ්ඨ සමාජයක්). A system of government with the three branches — the executive, the legislative and the judiciary was in action.

However, internal and external political movement with accompanied terrorism and violence intervened. It was ethnicity based in case of the multiple Tamil terrorist groups. It was political ideology based in case of the JVP.  The leaders and combatants in both cases came from the middle class and poor segments of the social hierarchy.  The elite tried to manipulate both towards their own ends.

It must be noted that the leader of the JVP, Rohana Wijeweera had been imprisoned during their first insurrection in 1971.  President Jayawardene pardoned and released him back into society.  The LTTE which became dominant among the Tamil terrorist groups was fighting for a separate state for the Tamils.

President Jayewardene articulated a policy that provided guidance in managing the situation with India who trained and supported the terrorists and the larger diplomatic efforts with the rest of the world.  The unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity of Sri Lanka was not up for negotiation.

The JVP terrorism and the LTTE terrorism were brought under control with the death of JVP leader Rohana Wijeweera in 1989 and the death of the LTTE leader Velupillai Prabhakaran in 2009.   Peace and stability were not available for more than three decades.

While the terrorist violence was brought under control, the root causes of the origination of these movements need to be throughly understood and addressed. Corruption at the top makes it very easy for any marginalized group to resort to revolution and violence to overthrow the existing regime. A new vision must capture the solution to transition into to a better power structure.

> **ETHICAL DEVELOPMENT&trade;**    
> **ධාර්මික සංවර්ධනය&trade;** 

The better we understand life’s purpose, individual purpose and the purpose of each institution, the better our effectiveness in rising up to the challenges.

> If the misery of the poor be caused not by the laws of nature, but by our institutions, great is our sin.  
> **Charles Darwin**

Cleaning up the top from greed, prejudice and corruption is needed to establish a robust foundation.  As mentioned earlier, the top and the bottom are quite coupled and related.  The clean up must be for the whole of society.  How to proceed is the challenge.  The Commander in Chief must become clean himself and facilitate the process.

This requires education and preparation.  God prepares our rulers and the citizens alike if we seek his guidance in faith.
