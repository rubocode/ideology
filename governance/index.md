## Governance

Why is governance necessary? Because no one is born with full knowledge of how to operate in the world.  Some never learn. Governance is different from running a business. In a business you can hire and fire as you please and the fired individual is to be taken care of by some other business or no one. Governance does not have the luxury of abandoning an individual into emptiness.

If you have a family and you have kids, you are not running a business, you are looking after everyone in the family — governing. What you need to do is empowering the family members to govern themselves. But as the leader and shepherd, you play an important role — primarily good guidance.  Governing a country is a much heavier endeavor that requires much more preparation.

> The best government is that which teaches us to govern ourselves.  
> **Johann Wolfgang von Goethe**

J. R. Jayewardene, as the Finance Minister of Ceylon, delivered a historic speech at the San Francisco Peace Conference on September 6, 1951. Jayewardene advocated for a free, independent, and unpunished Japan.  He successfully shifted the global conference from a stance of wartime vengeance to one of reconciliation.  He quoted the Dhammapada and changed the course of history for Japan.
