## God is Truth

God is Truth. To believe in God is to believe in Truth. Jesus Christ, the son of God, became flesh and came to Earth and now we have seen God and heard from Him. He died for our sins and was resurrected and was exalted to the highest place.

> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.  
> If ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also: and from henceforth ye know him, and have seen him.  
> **John 14:6-7 KJV**

> But seek the kingdom of God, and all these things shall be added to you.  
> Do not fear, little flock, for it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the kingdom.  
> **Luke 12:31-32 KJV**

> And all things are of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation.  
> **2 Corinthians 5:18 KJV**

I am not asking you to take my word on faith. I am simply stating where I have arrived.  My journey was long, complicated, difficult and full of confusion. I was a seeker and a Buddhist since my childhood and that helped — I was never an atheist. But a living God was necessary to redeem me from my confusion and guide me through the wilderness.

I had reached a desperate situation and I had no other option but to ask God for help. I went through the experience several times and an accidental meeting with a Christian pastor redirected my journey. I accepted Jesus Christ as my savior in May, 2019. I was baptized in November, 2022.

The thing with the Christian journey is that you have to believe first to see it. The worldly wisdom is that you’ve got to see it to believe it. You don’t need faith to believe what you see. The Christian Gospel is that you are saved by faith in Christ Jesus.

> For we walk by faith, not by sight.  
>**2 Corinthians 5:7 NKJV**

I am not going to elaborate any further as I am not trying to convince you to take the path that I chose. To the person who believes, you don’t need to convince them. To the person who doesn’t want to believe, my purpose here is not to present any argument.

What I intend to do is to describe the fundamental ideologies using Christian vocabulary so that if and when you care enough about the matter at hand you will remember hearing from me and have a leg up in seeking the answers yourself.
