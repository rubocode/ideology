## Systems

Any thing or an idea that is active is a system. It has an aim or a purpose. It creates and maintains either order or chaos. Order creates clarity and alignment and chaos promotes confusion and decay.

An individual is a system. Each individual has a physical body and a metaphysical aspect consisting of a mind, a soul and a spirit. Each individual is unique and his or her beliefs are also unique.  The knowledge and gifts each individual has are also unique to him or her.

A family and a community are also systems. A family is bound by blood while a community is any collection of individuals. A nation is a community that shares a particular geology and is governed by the same system of laws.  Significantly, a nation in the long term cultivates and nourishes a unique gene pool.

Belief systems are special. Think of a belief system as an Operating System. Some are simple and some are complex. Some are elegant and some are crude. Some are sophisticated and some are primitive.  Some are resilient and some are fragile. Some are useful and some are inadequate. Some are good and some are evil.

Belief systems are software. An individual is capable of understanding multiple belief systems. He or she can also choose to accept or reject them and may choose to practice them. If there is more than one individual that has accepted the same system of belief we call that community a tribe. You can belong to multiple tribes. A community that accommodates multiple tribes is called a culture.

A tribe can be somewhat primitive or restrictive.  It expects and respects everyone who is the same.  A culture welcomes multiple tribes that enriches life for everyone.  A culture is an operating system essentially more sophisticated and accommodating than a tribe.

The ultimate goal for an individual is to gain the capacity that allows them to see through any belief with wisdom and discernment — to be able to separate the wheat from the chaff. You refine your belief system along your journey and your discernment is your gain.  Either you understand or you don’t. This is what is called achieving mastery. This is enlightenment, the sacred goal of Buddhism.
